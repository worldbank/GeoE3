# WBL Index Reference

!!! abstract "Learning objective"
    By the end of this page you'll have a ready-to-use default score for
    three of the four Contextual-dimension factors (Workplace
    Discrimination, Regulatory Frameworks, Financial Inclusion) for any
    country in the world, without having to look each one up yourself in
    the World Bank's Women, Business and the Law (WBL) data.

[Getting Data](datacollection.md#tutorial-manual-national-index-score-entry)
explains that the four Contextual-dimension factors (EPLEX Score,
Workplace Discrimination, Regulatory Frameworks, Financial Inclusion)
are each a single national score you research and type in. Three of
those four come from WBL; the table below gives you that score directly
for every country WBL covers, so you only need to do your own research
for EPLEX Score (not a WBL indicator) or for a country that isn't listed.

## How each column is derived

WBL scores eight separate topics per country (Mobility, Workplace, Pay,
Marriage, Parenthood, Entrepreneurship, Assets, Pension), each 0–100.
GeoE3's three WBL-sourced Contextual factors map onto those topics like
this:

| GeoE3 factor | WBL topic(s) used |
|---|---|
| Workplace Discrimination | Workplace |
| Regulatory Frameworks | Average of Pay and Parenthood |
| Financial Inclusion | Entrepreneurship |

This matches the mapping already described for each factor in
[Getting Data](datacollection.md) and the
[Legacy Detailed Reference](../about/legacy-detailed-reference.md#workplace-discrimination).

!!! note
    WBL restructured its methodology starting with the 2026 report
    ("WBL 2.0"), scoring three pillars (legal frameworks, supportive
    frameworks, enforcement perceptions) per topic instead of one flat
    score. The topic-level scores this table uses are only published
    under the earlier ("1.0") methodology, which is why this table is
    pinned to the 2024 report year rather than the latest one.

!!! success "Recap"
    - Three of GeoE3's four Contextual factors come straight from WBL:
      Workplace Discrimination (WBL Workplace), Regulatory Frameworks
      (average of WBL Pay and Parenthood), Financial Inclusion (WBL
      Entrepreneurship).
    - The table above gives the 2024 report year score for every country
      WBL covers. For a country not listed, or a different report year,
      go to [wbl.worldbank.org](https://wbl.worldbank.org/en/wbl) directly.
